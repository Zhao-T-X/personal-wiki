# Idea Standard v1.0

## Definition

An Idea is a source-derived proposal, suggestion, hypothesis, possible solution, improvement direction, design direction or research direction that is not presented by the source as an established fact.

## Rules

- Ideas must come from the source.
- The model must not invent Ideas.
- Do not turn established facts into Ideas.
- Do not turn model-generated recommendations into Ideas.
- Do not turn concrete scheduled events into Ideas.
- Keep one coherent idea per item.

## Status

```text
candidate
accepted
implemented
rejected
archived
```

## Evidence

Every Idea requires source evidence.
